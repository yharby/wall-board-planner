Pegboard wall planner

Units

Drag boards from the tray onto your wall. They snap to the wall edges, the centre line and each other, and the measurements update as you move them.

    Tap a tile to add a board next to the others, or drag it to an exact spot. Drag a board to move it, tap it to see its exact gaps and drill marks. Shift-click to select several. Hold Alt while dragging to turn off snapping. Arrow keys move by 1 cm (Shift for 10 cm), Delete removes, Ctrl+D duplicates.

    Wall and desk

    Wall colour

    Drawer units are the light boxes on the desk. Drag them sideways to where they really stand, then click one to change its size.

    Drilling

    From the board maker’s assembly instructions, each board hangs on one rail with two wall screws, 20 cm, 40 cm or 60 cm apart for the 36, 56 or 76 cm board, so each screw sits 8 cm from the board’s side edge. Neighbouring boards leave a 0.5 cm joint (rails 16.5 cm apart side by side, 56.5 cm apart when stacked).

    This one is not printed in the instructions. The 6 cm default is read off the step 4 drawing, where the rail is fixed through the slot about 6 cm below the board’s top edge. Before drilling, fit the rail to a board, measure from the top edge down to the centre of the rail’s wall-screw holes, and enter it here.
